How it works

SortParcels are ordered by deadline, then priority, with merge sort.O(n log n)
Pack0/1 knapsack finds the best load under the weight limit and scores you against it.O(n·W)
RouteDijkstra gives road distances. Nearest-neighbour sets the benchmark route you try to beat.O(V²) and O(k²)

Scoring

Load score is your total priority divided by the optimal knapsack priority. Route score is the nearest-neighbour length divided by your length, capped at 100%. Your round score is the average of the two.
